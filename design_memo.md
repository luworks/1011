# Policy-Grounded Agent — Design Memo

**Purpose.** Demonstrate that a billing workflow applies the governing outage exception and declines unsupported tax conclusions. The CLI exposes customer turns, retrieved source excerpts, model candidate decisions, final decisions, and missing checks. Offline playback uses explicitly recorded fixtures; live runs use official OpenAI or Anthropic APIs. No real credit or external escalation is performed.

**Grounded replies.** Rule 3 and KB-1004/1005 support the supplied outage calculation: $225 ÷ 30 × 11 = **$82.50**, without multiplying the three-line monthly total again. Rule 3 excludes Rule 2's $75 goodwill cap. The request is within 30 calendar days after restoration; the separate 60-day statement window cannot replace that test. These conclusions depend on the supplied historical agent's NOC confirmation, labeled as transcript evidence, not a live lookup. Missing dispute-history/fraud checks prevent a claim that credit issuance is authorized. Decimal half-up rounding and elapsed full days are documented implementation conventions.

**Deliberately unresolved.** KB-1004 defines a plan-fee calculation base; KB-1003 describes taxes but does not say whether an outage credit recalculates them. The workflow answers the supported base question and explicitly declines to infer a tax adjustment. Human clarification is requested under a workflow evidence-gap rule, not an invented Nimbus tax policy or routing queue. A need for review is not a completed handoff.

**Hardest hierarchy.** The narrow outage exception replaces the general cap, but not the global escalation constraints. Reviewed executable rules therefore separate eligibility, arithmetic, and action authority. Unknown is a distinct condition, never equivalent to false. No ticket, an unverifiable ticket, and a ticket not yet checked are different situations. Policy conclusions are rendered from bounded templates; retrieved citations alone are insufficient. All ten KB articles are retained, with source positions and corpus hashes binding rules to reviewed text.

**Measured repair (v8).** `examples/workflow-v8/` preserves a real six-turn Haiku 5.5 adaptive/medium replay: seven scoped checks passed, zero technical failures, 13 calls, estimated US$0.011434, and 63.154 seconds summed turn time. The bill item is retained; the tax follow-up does not reopen an old question; amount, deadline, and no-issuance checks pass. The live/benchmark snapshot remains workflow hash `2a561f1…`; integrated post-freeze diagnostics changed the workspace to `8ce60a1…`, which has not been live-rerun. Fact inventory/current-request provenance, a narrow quoted bill-item fallback, and explicit-null verification revocation address general defects. Tax unknown is enforced by the native coverage schema, not evidence that an unconstrained model learned the policy. Candidate amount/status/action/policy-ID mismatches remain visible and gated. Historical examples retain their versions and do not validate v8. The immutable report is the 12:54:59 PDT checkpoint (62/84 screening runs); fresh/high acceptance and final model selection remain pending. A separate frozen-v5 policy experiment completed 120 pairs per model: Sol/Luna raw composite improved 85→100 / 63→75, while final composite fell 105→104 / 95→94. No policy promotion is justified.

**Measurement plan.** Compare model plus thinking configurations separately using independent development screening and matched fresh held-out tests. Preserve exact workflow, policy, charter, input, and oracle versions; retire holdouts used for tuning. Measure raw candidate and final correctness separately, escalation recall, answer completion, unsupported claims, end-to-end turn P95/P50, and cost per successful conversation. Native schema restrictions limit what “raw” claims can demonstrate. Expected answers remain outside model inputs; incomplete coverage and API failures remain in the reported denominator.

**Next step and limits.** Two retained utility failures involve background-reference request scope and ambiguous bill-item labels; diagnostics do not repair them. A further 27 charter attacks passed locally, not as live-model evidence. Freeze prompts before evaluating held-out cases, manually adjudicate semantic disagreements, and replace holdouts used for tuning. Rank accuracy first (including critical errors), then end-to-end turn P95/P50, then price. Screen configurations on development cases and run matched frozen tests only for finalists. Incomplete comparisons cannot establish a global winner. This is a finite experiment, not proof of zero hallucination. Source-quote checks still cannot fully validate semantic fact extraction or intent coverage; those residual risks motivate broader human-labeled multi-turn evaluation before any production integration.
