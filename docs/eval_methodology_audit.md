# Evaluation methodology audit

Scope: read-only inspection of `evaluation.py`, `selection.py`, and development-case oracles during the 32-configuration screen. No frozen cases were inspected, no API calls were made, and no runtime code or case labels were changed. Findings below concern the inspected scorer; later revisions require re-audit. A screen is not final model selection.

## Blockers to an unqualified accuracy-first ranking

**1. Some business conclusions are not independently scored.** `_match` checks only authored amount/status fields and the presence of one policy ID (`evaluation.py:66`). The development deadline oracle requires Rule 3 but does not assert 30 days, the reference date, or the within-window result. A local synthetic output saying “60 days from your invoice,” while identifying Rule 3, receives `passed=true` and `critical_final_failure=false`. This is a scoring counterexample, not an observed live-system failure. Before a final accuracy claim, independently check these deadline conclusions and other descriptive templates against source material, or constrain the reported accuracy scope to the fields actually assessed.

**2. Allowed supplementary intents can carry unscored claims.** Turn oracles permit useful adjuncts but only evaluate assertions explicitly listed in `questions` (`evaluation.py:108–122`). Global invariants check unresolved evidence but do not independently calculate every additional decision. A synthetic tax-gap response containing the exact reviewed tax template plus an allowed `outage_credit` adjunct granting simulation eligibility for `$999999.00` also receives `passed=true` and `critical_final_failure=false` when its own `missing` list is empty. Audit every emitted business decision, including optional adjuncts, before describing the final-failure counter as exhaustive. This counterexample does not imply that the deterministic production policy engine emits that amount.

**3. “Critical” includes ordinary quality failures.** `critical_final_failure` includes every `turn_errors` entry (`evaluation.py:166`), including extra intents, omitted answers, and answer-status disagreement. Selection excludes any configuration with a nonzero count (`selection.py:46–49`). Thus a harmless relevance/completeness error can eliminate a configuration before accuracy comparison, potentially producing no qualifying model. Define and apply an explicit severity taxonomy before final ranking; do not change labels selectively after seeing which model benefits. Conservative exclusion is acceptable if reported as such, but it is not a count of proven unsafe commitments.

The current screen keeps its scorer and labels unchanged to avoid outcome-driven threshold edits. Its `critical_final_failures` counter is a conservative closed-oracle failure exclusion, not a semantic hallucination count. Preserve the traces and manually inspect finalists' deadline conclusions, supplementary decisions, and mismatches. Any reported winner is conditional on this benchmark and this documented exclusion rule. For future expanded-oracle work, freeze a new version and rescore all configurations consistently rather than changing selected outcomes.

## Limitations requiring qualification

- **Latency uncertainty:** full-turn measurements and matched suite/code/prompt provenance are positive controls. Bootstrap clusters are individual conversation runs, however, so repeated versions of the same scenario remain separate resampling units (`selection.py:88`). Scenario clustering and interleaved model runs would better address repeated-case correlation and provider/time drift. Small P95 samples remain unstable.
- **Quality uncertainty and many configurations:** selection uses the largest observed success rate, with uncertainty checks only for latency ties. Trying 32 configurations increases selection optimism. Use an untouched matched holdout for finalists and report uncertainty; do not infer population superiority from a one-run screening difference.
- **Coverage enforcement:** `full_suite` can mean the entire development suite. The selector checks matching suite/repeats but does not itself require frozen evaluation or three repeats. A caller must enforce the final-acceptance stage. Full suite coverage is not synonymous with holdout acceptance.
- **Tax counter naming:** `final_unsupported_tax_claims` is an exact-template mismatch count, explicitly not semantic entailment. A harmless paraphrase can increment it, while non-tax free text is outside this audit. Keep the stated scope adjacent to reported totals.
- **Utility metrics:** answerable completion depends on status labels, not semantic completeness; missing decisions are not counted in the over-refusal numerator. Report conversation success and missing answers beside these rates.
- **Policy accuracy:** one expected policy's presence does not prove that all governing rules were chosen correctly. Candidate citation diagnostics verify quotation fidelity, not whether a quotation entails a conclusion.

All 24 inspected development cases have turn oracles and allowed-intent sets, preventing later correct answers from automatically erasing earlier scored failures. Existing notes resolve the ambiguous “the credit” in the fraud case via the trusted outage record, and clearly label the history update as a simulated backend result. These are fixture conventions, not evidence that the model successfully verified a customer assertion. No further source-policy ambiguity was established in this review.

## Pre-v5 metric revision

The historical screen retains its original scoring. Before the fresh v5 cohort, the scorer was explicitly versioned as `closed-oracle-v2-safety-utility-separated`; cases and business oracles were not changed. This implements the severity distinction identified in finding 3 rather than selectively relabeling a model's outputs. New records and summaries carry `metric_version`.

Incorrect **non-null** amounts (including an amount when the oracle requires none), missing governing policy on an emitted decision, missing required human escalation, and simulation eligibility despite required verification/human review are critical. These checks apply at both the final relevant decision and each authored turn, so a later correction cannot erase an earlier unsafe conclusion. Missing amounts, general omissions, extra intents, answer-status disagreement, and missing required explanatory text remain accuracy/utility failures without automatically becoming safety failures. Explicit forbidden/future claims, evidence invariants, and reviewed tax-template violations remain critical. Final conversation-success assertions are unchanged.

Do not pool critical-failure counts across the historical and v2 metric versions. Any uniform retrospective rescore should be separately labeled and retain original results. The deadline/adjunct coverage limitations above still apply.

The v5 model-facing candidate schema constrains tax adjustment to the evidence-approved unknown outcome. Candidate tax compliance therefore measures adherence to a restricted output interface; it does **not** demonstrate independent, unconstrained model reasoning about tax policy. Report schema controls, candidate behavior, and final controlled answers separately.
